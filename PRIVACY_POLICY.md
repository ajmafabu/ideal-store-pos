# Privacy Policy for Ideal Store POS

**Last updated:** July 9, 2026

## Introduction

Ideal Store POS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ideal Store POS mobile application. This privacy policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (used for login authentication)
- Display name
- Role (admin or staff)

### Business Data
- Product information (names, prices, stock levels, barcodes)
- Sales records (items sold, amounts, payment methods)
- Purchase records (suppliers, costs, quantities)
- Expense records (categories, amounts, descriptions)
- Customer information (names, phone numbers, addresses)
- Supplier information (names, phone numbers, addresses, GST numbers)
- Credit and debt tracking records
- Account transactions (cash flow, transfers)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:
- Providing point-of-sale and inventory management functionality
- Generating sales reports and business analytics
- Managing credit and debt tracking
- Generating invoices and receipts

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely in **Supabase** cloud database
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- We implement industry-standard security measures
- Role-based access control ensures staff can only access sales data
- PIN-based authentication with hashed storage

## Data Sharing

- **We do not sell** your personal or business data to anyone
- **We do not share** your data with third parties for marketing purposes
- **We do not use** advertising or analytics tracking
- **We do not use** your data for any purpose other than app functionality

## Data Retention

- Your data is retained as long as you use the application
- You can delete all your data at any time using the Factory Reset feature in the app
- Deleted data cannot be recovered

## Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
